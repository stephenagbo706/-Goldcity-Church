How Can We Pray for You?

You are not alone. Share your prayer request with us, and our GoldCity Prayer Team will stand with you in prayer. When someone prays for your request, we'll send you a text to let you know.

And when we pray for you, you'll know.
Each time our prayer team prays over your request, you will receive a brief text message letting you know that someone at GoldCity has prayed for you.
